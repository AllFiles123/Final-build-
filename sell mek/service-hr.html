FAQ

Human Resources — Common Questions

Everything you need to know before we start working on your people operations.

How long does a typical HR engagement take?

Most HR modernization engagements run 6–10 weeks, from audit and policy design through rollout and manager training, depending on your team size.

Do you work with our existing HR software?

Yes. We integrate with the HRIS, payroll and ATS tools you already use and only recommend new tooling when it clearly improves the process.

Can you help with a single hiring pipeline, not the full HR system?

Absolutely. Talent acquisition support can be scoped as a standalone project if that's the most pressing priority right now.

How is pricing structured for this service?

Pricing depends on team size and scope — book a meeting and we'll walk you through a plan that fits your stage and budget.
